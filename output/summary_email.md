Subject: Contract risk summary for TechVendor Ltd. agreement: High risk, gate FAIL
Overall risk is High because the contract contains P1 clauses, led by the unilateral assignment of Customer Data to Vendor in Section 6.1.
P1 finding: Section 6.1 assigns all Customer Data, including aggregated, derived and anonymised data, to Vendor for any commercial purpose Vendor deems appropriate, and Vendor may retain it after termination.
P1 finding: Sections 8.1 and 8.2 leave Customer liability uncapped, including for consequential and punitive damages, while Vendor's liability is capped at fees paid in the one month preceding the claim.
Recommended action: before signing, limit Vendor's use of Customer Data in Section 6.1 to providing the Services, and add a Customer liability cap in Section 8.2 while removing punitive damages from Section 8.1.
