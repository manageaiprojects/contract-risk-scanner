# Contract Risk Report — TechVendor Ltd. (input/sample_contract.txt)

## Risk Rating
**High** — Section 8.2 makes Customer's liability "uncapped" while capping Vendor's aggregate liability at fees paid in the one month preceding the claim, which is a P1 clause (uncapped liability).

### Critical Summary
The most dangerous clause is Section 8.2, which leaves Customer's liability uncapped, and Section 8.1 extends that liability to "punitive" damages "without limitation as to amount." The most important Customer obligation is the USD 120,000 annual fee payable within thirty (30) days of invoice under Section 3.1, which Vendor may raise by up to 15% per contract year automatically under Section 3.2 without Customer's consent. Before signing, require a liability cap for Customer under Sections 8.1 and 8.2 and remove the assignment of Customer Data to Vendor in Section 6.1.

## P1 Critical Clauses
| Clause type | Excerpt (max 30 words) | Risk explanation |
|---|---|---|
| Auto-renewal without adequate notice | "unless either Party provides written notice of non-renewal no fewer than seven (7) days before the end of the then-current term." | The Agreement renews automatically for successive 12-month Renewal Terms. The non-renewal notice window is only 7 days before the end of the then-current term, so Customer can easily miss it and be bound for another 12 months. |
| Uncapped liability | "Customer's liability under this Agreement shall be uncapped." | Customer's liability has no ceiling. Section 8.2 caps Vendor's aggregate liability at fees paid in the one (1) month preceding the claim, so the liability regime is asymmetric in Vendor's favour. |
| Punitive penalties | "Customer shall be liable to Vendor for any and all losses, damages, costs and expenses, whether direct, indirect, consequential or punitive" | Customer may be liable for punitive damages, and for indirect and consequential losses, "without limitation as to amount" (Section 8.1). This is an open-ended exposure with no cap. |
| Unilateral IP or data assignment to vendor | "Customer hereby assigns to Vendor all right, title and interest in and to Customer Data, including all aggregated, derived and anonymised data" | Customer assigns ownership of its Customer Data and derived data to Vendor by this clause alone, with no consent step. Vendor may use it for "any other commercial purpose Vendor deems appropriate" and may retain and use it after termination. |

## P2 Major Clauses
| Clause type | Excerpt (max 30 words) | Risk explanation |
|---|---|---|
| Broad indemnification | "Customer shall indemnify and hold harmless Vendor from all third-party claims arising from Customer Data or Customer's use of the Services." | Customer gives a broad indemnity covering all third-party claims that arise from Customer Data or from its use of the Services. The clause is one-sided, as the Agreement contains no reciprocal indemnity from Vendor. |
| Price escalation trigger | "Vendor may increase the subscription fees at the start of each contract year by up to fifteen percent (15%) of the fees in effect for the preceding year." | Vendor may raise fees by up to 15% each contract year. Section 3.2 says the increase takes effect automatically on written notice and does not require Customer's consent or an amendment. Customer's budget is therefore exposed to unilateral increases. |
| Customer data ownership ambiguity | "Customer Data" means all data, records, files and information submitted, uploaded or generated by Customer or its users through the Services. | The Agreement defines Customer Data but does not expressly state who owns it. Section 6.1 separately assigns it to Vendor, and Section 6.2 only requires "reasonable" safeguards. The relationship between these provisions is unclear and could be read against Customer. |

## Key Obligations
### TechVendor Ltd.
- Shall use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Services available 99.5% of the time each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance (2.2).
- Shall implement reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect Customer Data from unauthorised access (6.2).
- Warrants that the Services will perform materially in accordance with the documentation (7.1).
- Any fee increase (up to 15% of the prior year's fees, at the start of each contract year) takes effect automatically upon written notice to Customer (3.2).
- Either Party may terminate for convenience only by giving the other Party thirty (30) days' prior written notice (5.1).
- Either Party's non-renewal requires written notice no fewer than seven (7) days before the end of the then-current term (4.2).

### Acme Corp
- Shall pay an annual subscription fee of USD 120,000, invoiced annually in advance and payable within thirty (30) days of the invoice date (3.1).
- Late payments shall accrue interest at 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower (3.3).
- Upon termination, shall pay all fees accrued through the effective date of termination; prepaid fees are non-refundable (5.3).
- Hereby assigns to Vendor all right, title and interest in and to Customer Data, including all aggregated, derived and anonymised data (6.1).
- Shall be liable to Vendor for all losses, damages, costs and expenses, whether direct, indirect, consequential or punitive, arising out of or relating to Customer's use of the Services, without limitation as to amount; Customer's liability under the Agreement shall be uncapped (8.1, 8.2).
- Shall indemnify and hold harmless Vendor from all third-party claims arising from Customer Data or Customer's use of the Services (8.3).
- Either Party may terminate for convenience only by giving the other Party thirty (30) days' prior written notice (5.1).
- Either Party's non-renewal requires written notice no fewer than seven (7) days before the end of the then-current term (4.2).
