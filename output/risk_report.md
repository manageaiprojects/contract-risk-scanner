# Contract Risk Report — TechVendor Ltd. (input/sample_contract.txt)

## Risk Rating
**High** — The contract contains multiple P1 clauses, most significantly the unilateral assignment of Customer Data to Vendor in Section 6.1, together with uncapped Customer liability (Sections 8.1–8.2) and automatic renewal with only a 7-day non-renewal notice window (Section 4.2).

### Critical Summary
Section 6.1 has Customer assign to Vendor all rights in Customer Data, including aggregated and derived data, for any commercial purpose Vendor deems appropriate, and lets Vendor keep using that data after termination. The most important Customer obligation is paying USD 120,000 per year in advance, due within 30 days of invoice, with prepaid fees non-refundable (Sections 3.1, 5.3), and Vendor may raise fees by up to 15% per contract year automatically on written notice (Section 3.2). Before signing, require Section 6.1 to be deleted or narrowed to a limited licence needed to provide the Services, make Customer's liability in Sections 8.1–8.2 capped and mutual with Vendor's one-month-fee cap, and extend the 7-day non-renewal notice period in Section 4.2.

## Key Obligations
### TechVendor Ltd.
- Shall use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Services available 99.5% of the time each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance (Section 2.2).
- Shall implement reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect Customer Data from unauthorised access (Section 6.2).
- Warrants that the Services will perform materially in accordance with the documentation (Section 7.1).
- May increase subscription fees at the start of each contract year by up to 15% of the prior year's fees; increases take effect automatically upon written notice to Customer, without Customer's consent or an amendment (Section 3.2).
- Must give at least 30 days' prior written notice to terminate for convenience (Section 5.1).
- If in material breach, must cure within 15 days of receiving notice, failing which the other Party may terminate immediately (Section 5.2).
- Must give written notice of non-renewal no fewer than seven (7) days before the end of the then-current term to prevent automatic renewal (Section 4.2).
- Submits to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in Delaware, under Delaware governing law (Section 9.1).

### Acme Corp
- Shall pay an annual subscription fee of USD 120,000, invoiced annually in advance and payable within thirty (30) days of the invoice date (Section 3.1).
- Late payments shall accrue interest at 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower (Section 3.3).
- Shall pay all fees accrued through the effective date of termination; prepaid fees are non-refundable (Section 5.3).
- Shall be liable to Vendor for all losses, damages, costs and expenses, whether direct, indirect, consequential or punitive, arising from Customer's use of the Services, without limitation as to amount; Customer's liability is uncapped (Sections 8.1 and 8.2).
- Shall indemnify and hold harmless Vendor from all third-party claims arising from Customer Data or Customer's use of the Services (Section 8.3).
- Hereby assigns to Vendor all right, title and interest in Customer Data, including aggregated, derived and anonymised data, for analytics, product development, benchmarking and any other commercial purpose Vendor deems appropriate (Section 6.1).
- Right to access and use the Services is non-exclusive, non-transferable, and limited to Customer's internal business purposes during the Subscription Term (Section 2.1).
- Must give at least 30 days' prior written notice to terminate for convenience (Section 5.1), and must give written notice of non-renewal no fewer than seven (7) days before the end of the then-current term to prevent automatic renewal (Section 4.2).
