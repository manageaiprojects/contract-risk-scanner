SOFTWARE-AS-A-SERVICE SUBSCRIPTION AGREEMENT

This Software-as-a-Service Subscription Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of January 15, 2026 (the "Effective Date") by and between:

TechVendor Ltd., a company organised under the laws of England and Wales, with its principal office at 42 Harbour Street, London EC2A 4BX, United Kingdom ("Vendor"); and

Acme Corp, a Delaware corporation, with its principal office at 1200 Market Avenue, Wilmington, DE 19801, USA ("Customer").

Vendor and Customer are each a "Party" and together the "Parties."

1. DEFINITIONS

1.1 "Services" means the cloud-based workflow automation platform known as "TechFlow Enterprise," together with any updates, support and related documentation made available by Vendor.

1.2 "Customer Data" means all data, records, files and information submitted, uploaded or generated by Customer or its users through the Services.

1.3 "Subscription Term" means the Initial Term and any Renewal Term, as defined in Section 4.

2. SERVICES AND ACCESS

2.1 Subject to the terms of this Agreement, Vendor grants Customer a non-exclusive, non-transferable right to access and use the Services during the Subscription Term solely for Customer's internal business purposes.

2.2 Vendor shall use commercially reasonable efforts to make the Services available 99.5% of the time each calendar month, excluding scheduled maintenance.

3. FEES AND PAYMENT

3.1 Customer shall pay Vendor an annual subscription fee of USD 120,000, invoiced annually in advance and payable within thirty (30) days of the invoice date.

3.2 Price Adjustments. Vendor may increase the subscription fees at the start of each contract year by up to fifteen percent (15%) of the fees in effect for the preceding year. Such increases shall take effect automatically upon written notice to Customer and shall not require Customer's consent or an amendment to this Agreement.

3.3 Late payments shall accrue interest at 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower.

4. TERM AND RENEWAL

4.1 This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an initial period of twelve (12) months (the "Initial Term").

4.2 Automatic Renewal. Upon expiry of the Initial Term, this Agreement shall automatically renew for successive twelve (12) month periods (each a "Renewal Term") unless either Party provides written notice of non-renewal no fewer than seven (7) days before the end of the then-current term.

5. TERMINATION

5.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by giving the other Party thirty (30) days' prior written notice.

5.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately upon written notice if the other Party materially breaches this Agreement and fails to cure such breach within fifteen (15) days of receiving notice.

5.3 Upon termination, Customer shall pay all fees accrued through the effective date of termination. Prepaid fees are non-refundable.

6.1 Customer hereby assigns to Vendor all right, title and interest in and to Customer Data, including all aggregated, derived and anonymised data, for the purposes of analytics, product development, benchmarking and any other commercial purpose Vendor deems appropriate. Vendor may retain and use such data after termination of this Agreement.

6.2 Vendor shall implement reasonable administrative and technical safeguards designed to protect Customer Data from unauthorised access.
